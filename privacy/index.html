Privacy.

What this website stores, in plain language.

The short version

This website does not collect personal information. There are no accounts, no forms that submit to us, no analytics, no advertising trackers and no third-party cookies.

What is stored on your device

When you confirm your age, we store two values in your browser’s local storage so the age screen does not reappear on every page:

  • sr-age-verified — that you confirmed you are of legal age
  • sr-age-province — the province or territory you selected

These never leave your device and are not sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Fonts

Typefaces on this site are served by Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google when a page loads. Google may log that request, including your IP address. We are working on serving the fonts ourselves to remove this.

If you email us

If you write to info@spaceracecannabis.com or ask to join our mailing list, we keep your message and address so we can reply and, if you asked for updates, send them. We do not sell or share your details. Ask us to delete you at any time and we will.
